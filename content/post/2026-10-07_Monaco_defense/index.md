[Francesco P. Monaco] has successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis
in Information Technologies for Engineering
entitled
*"Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces for Sub-6GHz and mm-Wave Applications to Smart Radio Environments"*,
supervised by Profs. [Giuseppe Castaldi](/author/giuseppe-castaldi), [Andrea Cusano](https://www.unisannio.it/it/users/acusano),
and [Vincenzo Galdi](/author/vincenzo-galdi).

Congratulations Dr. Monaco!
